# Forecast Compare (public, older version)

https://d3skj3t.github.io/oxley-forecast-public

The earlier public version of the forecast comparison app. It shows the featured locations from the
dropdown, comparing the Bureau of Meteorology, Weatherzone, WillyWeather, Elders Weather, Weather
Underground and Windy side by side.

Its location list and figures now come from the
[aus-forecast](https://github.com/D3skJ3t/aus-forecast) repository, whose scraper keeps them up to
date. This repository only holds the page (`index.html`). The newer app, with place search and
boating conditions, is at https://d3skj3t.github.io/aus-forecast

Figures belong to the sites they come from. For warnings and safety decisions, follow the Bureau of
Meteorology.
